# Abstract

The abstract states the result and how it is proved. The introduction defines the objects.

## Say

- The theorem, in ordinary words.
- A numerical cutoff exactly as proved. \(2^{22001}\) is not "about \(22000\)".
- When the argument is a conflict of bounds: for every case past that cutoff, a lower bound and an upper bound cannot hold at the same time. Every remaining case is ruled out by a direct computation, when that is the proof.
- After that conflict, one short sentence on the external result that sets the threshold. Name the authors and the year: Bauer and Bennett (2018) give a restricted irrationality exponent of about \(1.6149\), which lies just below the golden ratio, the threshold at which these bounds conflict. The form is name and year, as in the references skill.

## Do not say

- A word this paper does not define, in place of the result. This is a maths paper not an english essay or email. "We found that" and "emptiness" are not the result. State the result: for every such \(q\) at least \(2^{22001}\), a lower bound and an upper bound cannot hold at the same time.
- Definitions of the map, its variables, or other notation. That is the introduction.
- Names coined in the paper, such as "window" and "near miss", when the same fact can be said as a lower bound and an upper bound.
- That both bounds come from the external paper, or that each bound is built from both sources. State the conflict first. Then say what the cited result contributes.
